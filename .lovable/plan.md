# Premium editorial refinement

## Goal
Make the current Ahmedabad home-cleaning page roughly 50% simpler and more spacious without changing prices, business details, or adding content.

## Changes
- Preserve the existing hero image and core headline, but remove the hero price strip and shorten supporting text.
- Consolidate repeated pricing into one clear pricing section with separate 4-card grids for flats and bungalows.
- Replace repeated service explanations with two large editorial image sections for flat and bungalow cleaning.
- Reduce specialist services to a simple name-and-price list.
- Keep the before/after section visual-first with large imagery and only essential labels.
- Limit “Why Choose Us” to four short, divider-separated points and simplify “What We Clean.”
- Keep only the useful FAQs and shorten the final booking section to one sentence and two actions.
- Remove duplicated calls to action, decorative numbering, excessive labels, and placeholder-facing explanatory copy.
- Increase section spacing, image scale, and horizontal breathing room while retaining the off-white, charcoal, white, and restrained amber palette.

## Technical details
- Refactor only the existing home route and global visual tokens.
- Retain current images, anchor navigation, SEO metadata, pricing values, address, and conversion tracking.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, key navigation anchors, accordion behavior, and contact actions in the live preview.
